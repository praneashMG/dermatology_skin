Targeted Cosmetic Dermatology Procedures

Filter by category to explore indications, active clinical mechanisms, and expected recovery windows.

Injectable

Botox & Neuromodulators

Micro-dosed purified botulinum neurotoxin type A (Botox, Dysport, Daxxify). Softens glabellar frown lines, crow's feet, forehead furrows, and relaxes masseter muscles for facial contouring.

Downtime: Zero (20 min visit)
Longevity: 3 - 5 Months
Injectable

Hyaluronic Dermal Fillers

Vycross and XpresHAn cross-linked hyaluronic gels (Juvederm Voluma, Restylane Kysse/Defyne). Restores midface volume loss, sculpts cheekbones, smooths nasolabial folds, and refines lips with micro-cannula safety.

Downtime: 24 - 48 Hours Mild Swelling
Longevity: 12 - 18 Months
Ablative Laser

Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing

Gold standard 10,600nm micro-ablative laser creating controlled thermal injury columns. Drastically tightens lax skin, erases deep perioral lines, and levels severe atrophic acne scarring.

Downtime: 5 - 7 Days Peeling
Longevity: Multi-Year Remodeling
Picosecond Laser

PicoSure Pro 755nm Laser

Ultra-short picosecond photomechanical acoustic pressure waves shatter stubborn melanin deposits and tattoo ink into dust-like particles without excessive thermal collateral damage.

Downtime: Minimal (2 - 4 Hours Erythema)
Sessions: 3 - 4 Treatments
Clinical Facial

Medical HydraFacial MD Elite

Four-in-one patented vortex technology: simultaneous deep pore exfoliation, painless glycolic extraction, peptide infusion, and LED light therapy customized with physician-strength growth factor boosters.

Downtime: None (Immediate Glow)
Frequency: Monthly Maintenance
RF Energy

Morpheus8 Subdermal Remodeling

Fractionated radiofrequency delivered through micro-pins up to 4mm deep into the subdermal adipose matrix. Tightens lower jowls, lifts submental laxity, and reorganizes collagen architecture.

Downtime: 2 - 3 Days Mild Pinkness
Longevity: 18+ Months
