Clinical Conditions

Medical Dermatology Specializations

From stubborn acne and complex autoimmune dermatoses to full-body skin cancer screenings, our dermatologists formulate precise therapeutic plans.

Acne Specialist

Acne Vulgaris & Cystic Acne

Multimodal protocols targeting sebum overproduction, *C. acnes* colonization, and follicular hyperkeratinization. Treatments include medical retinoids, hormonal modulators, and managed isotretinoin courses.

Treatments: Retinoids, Spironolactone, Isotretinoin
Vascular Redness

Rosacea & Facial Erythema

Specialized care for erythematotelangiectatic, papulopustular, and ocular rosacea. Utilizing pulsed-dye vascular lasers, topical ivermectin, oxymetazoline, and barrier recovery regimens.

Treatments: Pulsed Dye Laser, Topical Ivermectin
Chronic Pruritus

Eczema & Atopic Dermatitis

Evidence-based management for severe chronic pruritus and barrier dysfunction. Advanced biologics (Dupilumab/Tralokinumab), JAK inhibitors, non-steroidal topicals, and wet-wrap protocols.

Treatments: Biologics, JAK Inhibitors, Barrier Repair
Autoimmune Derm

Psoriasis & Plaque Disorders

Comprehensive therapy for plaque, guttate, and inverse psoriasis. Including targeted IL-17/IL-23 inhibitor biologics, systemic immunosuppressants, and Narrowband UVB phototherapy.

Treatments: IL-17/23 Biologics, Narrowband UVB
Cancer Screening

Skin Cancer Screening & Mole Mapping

High-magnification polarized dermoscopy, digital surveillance of atypical dysplastic nevi, early detection of Melanoma, Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC), and Squamous Cell Carcinoma (SCC).

Procedures: Digital Mole Mapping, Shave/Punch Biopsy
Scalp & Hair

Alopecia & Scalp Disorders

Comprehensive trichoscopy and scalp biopsy for androgenetic alopecia, alopecia areata, telogen effluvium, and scarring alopecias. Systemic JAK inhibitors and autologous growth factor injections.

Treatments: Trichoscopy, Intralesional Injections, PRP
In-Clinic Diagnostics

Advanced Diagnostic Equipment & Lab Capabilities

Same-Day Histopathology & Biopsy

In-house laboratory partnership for rapid microscopic examination of atypical cells, infectious pathogens, and autoimmune rashes.

Polarized Digital Dermoscopy

High-resolution subsurface pigmentation visualization allowing precise non-invasive distinction between benign nevi and malignant lesions.

Medical Narrowband UVB Booths

Calibrated wavelength therapeutic phototherapy for vitiligo, severe eczema, and generalized plaque psoriasis with minimal erythema risk.

Facility Spotlight

Sterile Minor Procedure Surgical Suites

Equipped for outpatient dermatologic surgery, cyst excisions, and electrodessication.

Patient Pathway

What to Expect During Your Medical Visit

A thorough, unhurried consultation focused on diagnostic clarity and measurable improvement.

1

Clinical History

Deep-dive into symptom timelines, previous medication responses, family genetics, and lifestyle triggers.

2

Full Exam & Dermoscopy

Detailed visual and dermoscopic inspection of all affected anatomical sites with magnification.

3

Biopsy / Diagnostics

Same-day minor punch or shave biopsy under local anesthetic if histological verification is required.

4

Prescription & Care

Personalized topical, systemic, or biologic regimen with digital follow-up through our patient portal.
