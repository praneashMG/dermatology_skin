Advanced Clinical Technology & Devices

We invest exclusively in FDA-cleared, golden-standard dermatological platforms to guarantee micro-precision calibration, maximum patient safety, and optimal clinical efficacy.

Ablative Skin Resurfacing

Fractional CO2 (10,600 nm)

Ablative micro-thermal treatment columns for deep acne scars, advanced rhytids, and surgical scar revision with computer-patterned scanner control.

Picosecond Photomechanical

PicoSure® Pro (755 nm)

Picosecond laser targeting stubborn melasma, hyperpigmentation, and multi-color tattoo removal without thermal damage to surrounding tissue.

Vascular Pulsed Dye

Candela Vbeam® (595 nm)

Gold standard for rosacea, facial telangiectasias, port-wine stains, cherry angiomas, and active erythema with integrated dynamic cooling (DCD).

Fractional RF Subdermal

Morpheus8™ RF Microneedling

Subdermal adipose remodeling device penetrating up to 4mm to coagulate subdermal fat and stimulate profound collagen contraction.

Digital Diagnostics

Polarized Digital Dermoscopy

Epiluminescence microscopy with 40x optical magnification and polarized filters to detect early melanoma lesions and dysplastic nevi.

Medical Phototherapy

Narrowband UVB Cabinets (311 nm)

Dosimetry-controlled ultraviolet light cabins for steroid-sparing systemic management of severe psoriasis, vitiligo, and atopic dermatitis.

Medical Vortex Hydration

HydraFacial™ MD Elite

Patented Vortex-Fusion delivery system for hydradermabrasion, painless beta-hydroxy acid extractions, and antioxidant infusion.

Surgical Sterility

Class B Autoclaves & HEPA Suites

Hospital-standard positive-pressure procedure rooms and fractionated vacuum sterilizers ensuring zero cross-contamination risk.
