Medical Solutions

Medical Dermatology Care

Evidence-based diagnostic and therapeutic protocols formulated by board-certified physicians for chronic and acute skin, hair, and scalp disorders.

Medical Care

1. Acne & Acne Scarring

Multimodal protocols targeting the 4 underlying pathophysiological drivers: follicular hyperkeratinization, excess sebum, *C. acnes* colonization, and inflammation. Includes medical-grade topical retinoids, hormonal modulators (Spironolactone), managed courses of oral isotretinoin, and subcision/TCA Cross for atrophic icepick and boxcar scars.

Therapies: Isotretinoin, Retinoids, TCA Cross
From $120 / Consult Book Care
Medical Care

2. Eczema & Atopic Dermatitis

Comprehensive management for severe chronic pruritus, skin barrier breakdown, and lichenification. Protocols feature targeted IL-4/IL-13 receptor antagonist biologics (Dupilumab/Tralokinumab), non-steroidal PDE4 and JAK inhibitors, customized soak-and-seal lipid replacement, and wet-wrap barrier healing therapy.

Therapies: Biologics, JAK Inhibitors, Lipids
From $110 / Consult Book Care
Medical Care

3. Psoriasis Management

Targeted treatment for plaque, guttate, inverse, and erythrodermic psoriasis. We formulate individualized pathways combining Narrowband UVB 311nm phototherapy booths, oral phosphodiesterase-4 inhibitors, and next-generation IL-17 and IL-23 subcutaneous biologics achieving up to 90-100% skin clearance (PASI-90).

Therapies: Narrowband UVB, IL-17/23 Biologics
From $150 / Consult Book Care
Allergy / Diagnostics

4. Skin Allergies & Patch Testing

Identification and desensitization for allergic contact dermatitis and chronic urticaria (hives). Utilizing comprehensive 80-allergen T.R.U.E. patch test panels to pinpoint specific contact triggers (fragrances, preservatives, nickel, rubber chemicals) and medical biologic therapy (Omalizumab) for refractory hives.

Therapies: 80-Allergen Patch Test, Biologics
From $160 / Test Panel Book Care
Trichology

5. Hair Loss & Alopecia

Specialized clinical trichology for androgenetic alopecia (male/female pattern baldness), alopecia areata, telogen effluvium, and scarring alopecias. Diagnosis via high-magnification trichoscopy and scalp biopsy, followed by oral minoxidil/finasteride optimization, intralesional triamcinolone, and autologous PRP growth factors.

Therapies: Trichoscopy, Scalp PRP, JAK Inhibitors
From $175 / Consult Book Care
Scalp Health

6. Scalp Conditions & Folliculitis

Dedicated care for persistent seborrheic dermatitis, scalp psoriasis, bacterial/fungal folliculitis, and intense scalp pruritus. Treatments utilize compounded antifungal keratolytic rinses, non-greasy topical anti-inflammatory solutions, and anti-microbial photodynamic light therapy to restore a healthy follicular micro-environment.

Therapies: Keratolytics, Scalp Shampoos, Anti-Fungals
From $130 / Consult Book Care
Preventative Health

7. Total-Body Skin Checks & Cancer Screenings

Annual Exam

Complete head-to-toe polarized dermoscopy examination of every mole, freckle, and lesion. Early detection of Melanoma, Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC), and Squamous Cell Carcinoma (SCC). Includes digital mole mapping for high-risk patients, same-day suspicious lesion biopsies, and on-site Mohs micrographic surgical referrals.

Polarized Dermoscopy Digital Mole Mapping Same-Day Punch/Shave Biopsy

Covered by Most Insurance Plans

$220 / Insured Copay

Cosmetic Excellence

Cosmetic & Aesthetic Services

Physician-performed cosmetic dermatological enhancements designed to elevate natural facial architecture, boost neocollagenesis, and restore radiant skin vitality.

Exfoliation & Renewal

1. Medical Chemical Peels

Customized therapeutic chemical exfoliation designed by doctors to dissolve dull epidermal cells, stimulate basal cellular turnover, and correct stubborn pigmentation. We utilize clinical Glycolic (AHA), Salicylic (BHA), Modified Jessner's, and Medium-depth Trichloroacetic Acid (TCA) formulations tailored to your Fitzpatrick skin phototype.

Primary Indications: Melasma, Sun Damage, Comedonal Acne, Texture
Downtime: 2 - 5 Days of Gentle Peeling
Recommended Course: Series of 3 - 4 Sessions
Starting from $175
Book Chemical Peel
Collagen Remodeling

2. Medical Microneedling & RF

Precision micro-punctures delivered with medical pen systems or Morpheus8 subdermal radiofrequency needles up to 4mm depth. Stimulates intense natural wound-healing cascades, flooding the dermis with Type I and III collagen and elastin. Enhanced with topical growth factors, hyaluronic infusion, or autologous PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma).

Primary Indications: Atrophic Acne Scars, Enlarged Pores, Laxity, Stretch Marks
Downtime: 24 - 48 Hours Mild Erythema
Results: Progressive over 3 - 6 Months
Starting from $290
Book Microneedling
FDA-Cleared Devices

3. Advanced Laser Treatments

State-of-the-art optical and energy-based workstations operated exclusively by credentialed laser dermatologists. Includes Fractional CO2 (10,600nm) for deep wrinkles and severe scarring, PicoSure Pro (755nm) for non-thermal melasma and tattoo shattering, and Vbeam Pulsed Dye Laser (595nm) for rosacea and vascular telangiectasias.

Workstations: Fractional CO2, PicoSure, Vbeam Pulsed Dye
Downtime: 0 - 5 Days depending on modality
Operator: Board-Certified Dermatologist
Starting from $350
